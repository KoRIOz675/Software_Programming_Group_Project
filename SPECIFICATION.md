# Specification Document

## Teck stack

- Python
- Pygame

## Inspiration

- Old School RPG

## Features

- Graphical interface
- Pixelated art style
- Point & click
- Turn based combat
- Background / combat music

## Gameplay

- Medieval Fantasy world
- Multiple sprites for the hero: the player choose one
- The player choose the name of the hero
- The player gets points to separate in different statistics

## Combat mechanics

- turned base combat with initiative to determine the order of the round
- Dice based attack damage
- Armor class defense, if the result of the attacking roll is inferior to the defense the attack misses, else it hit with full damage of the weapon
- Three types of damage, strength weapon, dexterity weapon, magic spells.
- Each class will be link to a single type of damage.

## Art Style

The sprites and art styles are from [Anokolisa on itch.io](https://anokolisa.itch.io/)
